5

That’s it

From now on the Center opens like any other app. Sign in, pick your plugins and press Install - macOS asks for your Mac password once per install, because plugins go into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.

On macOS 14 Sonoma or older? It is quicker: Control-click RONE Plugins Center in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.
A plugin does not show up in your DAW? Rescan plugins once. In Logic: Audio FX → Audio Units → RonePlugins. In Ableton / FL Studio: the VST3 list under RonePlugins.
